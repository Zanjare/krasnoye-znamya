- First public release as Krasnoye Znamya (Red Banner Themes).

- No italics anywhere; bold marks structure (keywords, definitions, decorators, None/True/False).
- Every syntax color is checked: contrast of at least 4.5:1, comfortable text brightness, readable selections, distinct hues.
